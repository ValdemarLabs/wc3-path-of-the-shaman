# WC3 - Path of the Shaman
## README
*Last updated: 2026-08-07*

**Path of the Shaman (PotS)** is an open-world, story-driven single-player RPG for Warcraft III, with a strong focus on immersion, exploration, character progression, and a living game world.

The story follows **Nazgrek**, an orc shaman whose journey takes him across a large world filled with quests, factions, companions, dungeons, secrets, and hostile regions.

While inspired by Warcraft's universe, the story, locations, and characters in Path of the Shaman are fictional and **non-canonical to Warcraft's timeline**.

**Map size:** 480x480  
**Status:** Under heavy development

### Overview

Path of the Shaman combines familiar Warcraft III and RPG mechanics with a large number of custom systems built specifically for the project.

The map is designed primarily around a **single-player experience**. Exploration and side content play a major role, but the game remains story-driven: quests, dialogue choices, faction relationships, and player actions can affect how parts of the journey unfold.

Although many of the core ideas, systems, and terrain are already implemented, the project is still actively being developed and is subject to significant changes.

### Main Characters

- **Nazgrek** - the main playable character and an orc shaman
- **Zul'kis** - a major character and troll shaman 
- **Shadowclaw** - Nazgrek's initial wolf companion

The world also contains numerous side characters, quest givers, faction leaders, companions, enemies, and other recurring characters.

### Core Features

- **Hundreds of quests**, including main, optional, repeatable, and daily quests
- **Large interconnected world** divided into zones, subzones, settlements, and dungeons
- **Custom item, loot, rarity, inventory, and equipment systems**
- **Faction and reputation system** that affects hostility, dialogue, quests, trading, companions, and rewards
- **Character progression** Custom ability and talent progression with Elemental, Enhancement, Restoration, and Totemic specialization paths
- **Companion system** with recruitable AI heroes and multiple behaviour modes
- **Pet system** with tameable and independently levelled beasts
- **Crafting and gathering**, including dynamically spawning resources
- **Custom combat statistics**, including hit, critical strike, block, dodge, and spell power
- **Death and resurrection system** with selectable graveyards and resurrection penalties
- **Travel systems** using ships and flying wyverns
- **Arenas** with combat challenges and Arena Mark rewards
- **Dynamic environment**, including a long day-night cycle, weather, cold, and Frostbite mechanics
- **Custom RPG-style minimap** with a focused local view and enlarged map mode
- **Custom sounds and voice lines** for cinematics, heroes, companions, and combat

### Development

Path of the Shaman is largely developed as a solo project. Because of its scope, development is gradual and individual systems, areas, quests, balance, and presentation may change substantially before completion.

Much of the project is supported by custom JASS/vJASS systems covering AI, abilities and talents, quests and dialogue, companions, vendors, UI, environments, combat, events, and other RPG functionality.

Casual development videos, posts, screenshots, etc. may show ongoing work and experimentation, while larger milestones and updates may be announced separately.

Testing, bug reports, gameplay feedback, code review, proofreading, and help identifying missing resource credits are particularly useful to the project.
